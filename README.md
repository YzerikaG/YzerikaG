## 🔭 What I'm Currently Building

**Closet IQ** *(working title)* — a personal wardrobe inventory app.

Every 3–4 months, I go through the same thing: a full wardrobe spring clean, only to find clothes I haven't touched in years. So I'm building the solution myself.

This isn't just another closet app. It's a personal venture into:
- 📦 **Inventory tracking** — log what you own, what you've worn, what's been sitting untouched
- 🎨 **UI/UX experimentation** — exploring what makes a design both timeless and aesthetic
- 🤖 **AI-powered outfit suggestions** — so you never stare blankly at a full closet again

> *"The best projects are the ones that solve your own problems."*
